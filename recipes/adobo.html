Chicken Adobo

← Back to Home

Description

The national dish of the Philippines — tender chicken simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and bay leaves. Served over hot steamed rice, it's simply irresistible!

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs — 1 kg, bone-in and skin-on
  • Soy sauce — ½ cup
  • White vinegar — ⅓ cup
  • Garlic — 1 head, crushed
  • Onion — 1 medium, chopped
  • Bay leaves — 3–4 pieces
  • Whole peppercorns — 1 teaspoon
  • Water — 1 cup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine chicken, soy sauce, and garlic. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Transfer everything to a cooking pot. Add vinegar, onions, bay leaves, peppercorns, and water.
  3. Bring to a boil over medium heat. Once boiling, lower heat and cover.
  4. Simmer for 30–40 minutes until chicken is tender.
  5. Remove the lid and let the sauce reduce to your liking.
  6. Serve hot over plenty of steamed rice. Enjoy!
