Chicken Adobo

Description

The national dish of the Philippines — savory, tangy, and absolutely delicious!

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic and bay leaves
  • Peppercorns

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken in soy sauce and garlic
  2. Simmer with vinegar, bay leaves, and peppercorns
  3. Cook until chicken is tender
  4. Serve over hot rice
